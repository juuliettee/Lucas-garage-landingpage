Privacy & Legal Notice

Compliant with UK GDPR, Data Protection Act 2018, PECR, Electronic Commerce Regulations 2002, and Consumer Rights Act 2015.

Plain-English Summary

Lucas Garage is an independent local workshop in Walworth, London SE17. We only collect the minimal details needed to arrange your vehicle viewing or workshop quote. We do not use tracking pixels, we do not track you across other websites, and we never sell your personal data.

2. Personal Data & Privacy (UK GDPR Art. 13)

When you request a vehicle viewing or send a message via WhatsApp, telephone, or online booking, we process your name, contact details, and vehicle preferences:

  • Legal Basis: Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR (taking steps at your request prior to entering into a contract for vehicle inspection or service).
  • Data Retention: Viewing inquiry details are kept only for scheduling and deleted after 90 days if no purchase is made. Invoices and warranty records for purchased vehicles or repairs are retained for 6 years in accordance with UK HMRC tax and accounting laws.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or trade customer contact details with any third parties or marketing brokers.

3. Cookies & Tracking (PECR Regulation 6)

Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), websites must obtain consent before storing non-essential cookies.

Lucas Garage uses NO marketing cookies, NO advertising pixels, and NO behavioral trackers.

We only use Strictly Necessary and Functional Preference cookies which are exempt from consent under PECR Regulation 6(4)(b):

Cookie Category Purpose
session Strictly Necessary Maintains secure authenticated session for mechanic portal operations.
googtrans Functional Remembers your chosen language (English or Spanish) when you toggle the language switch.

Because no non-essential tracking cookies are used, an intrusive cookie banner pop-up is legally not required under PECR.

4. Consumer Rights & Workshop Guarantees (CRA 2015)

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, all second-hand vehicles sold must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose, and as described:

  • Workshop Labour Guarantee: Our 3-month in-house workshop labour guarantee is provided in addition to, and does not affect, your statutory consumer rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
  • Vehicle Transparency: All vehicles are inspected, and any known history (including insurance categories Cat N / Cat S where applicable) is disclosed openly prior to purchase.
  • Dispute Resolution: If an issue arises, we aim to resolve it directly and fairly at the workshop. Consumers also have the right to seek advice from the Citizens Advice Consumer Service.

5. Exercising Your Rights

You have the statutory right to request a copy of your personal data, request corrections, or request deletion at any time without charge.

Contact Luca directly by telephone or WhatsApp on 07535 321145, by email at lucasgarage@hotmail.co.uk, or visit the workshop in person. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
